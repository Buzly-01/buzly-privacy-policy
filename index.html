Buzly Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Welcome to Buzly ("Buzly", "we", "our", or "us"). Buzly is a local marketplace and booking platform that connects customers with nearby businesses such as salons, beauty parlours, tattoo studios, footwear stores, and other local shops.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Buzly mobile application ("App"). By creating an account or using Buzly, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide our services, improve the user experience, maintain platform security, and operate the Buzly marketplace.

1.1 Customer Information

When a customer creates an account, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Photo (if uploaded)
  • Authentication information through Google Sign-In or Email OTP
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for notifications
  • Booking history
  • Account creation date and related account information

Important: Buzly does not store your password in its own database. Authentication credentials are securely managed through our authentication provider (Supabase Authentication).

1.2 Shop Owner Information

When a business owner registers on Buzly, we may collect:

  • Owner Name
  • Email Address
  • Shop Name
  • Shop Address
  • City
  • District
  • Shop Location
  • Business Opening Time
  • Business Closing Time
  • Services Offered
  • Service Prices
  • Service Duration
  • Shop Banner Image
  • Shop Profile Image
  • Subscription Information
  • Account creation and update timestamps

This information is used to create and manage the shop profile displayed to customers.

1.3 Booking Information

When customers make bookings, Buzly collects information such as:

  • Selected Shop
  • Selected Services
  • Booking Date
  • Booking Time
  • Booking Status
  • Booking History
  • Total Booking Amount (if applicable)

This information helps both customers and shop owners manage appointments efficiently.

1.4 Images and Media

Users may upload images, including:

Customers

  • Profile Photo

Shop Owners

  • Shop Profile Image
  • Shop Banner Image

Uploaded images are securely stored and displayed only for the intended purpose within the Buzly platform.

1.5 Location Information

Buzly does not collect or track customers' live location.

For shop owners, we collect only the shop's location provided during shop registration. This location is displayed to customers so they can easily find the business.

We do not continuously track the owner's device location.

1.6 Notifications

Buzly uses push notifications to provide important updates, including:

  • Booking updates
  • Booking confirmations
  • Booking status changes
  • Important account-related notifications
  • Service-related announcements

Users can manage notification permissions through their device settings.

1.7 Payment Information

Buzly offers subscription plans for shop owners using Razorpay.

For subscription payments, payment processing is handled securely by Razorpay. Buzly may store limited payment-related information such as:

  • Razorpay Order ID
  • Razorpay Payment ID
  • Subscription Plan
  • Payment Status
  • Subscription Start Date
  • Subscription Expiry Date

Buzly does not store debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, CVV, or bank account credentials. All payment information is processed securely by Razorpay in accordance with their security standards.

1.8 Information Collected Automatically

To improve the reliability and security of the App, Buzly may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device Information
  • App Version
  • Operating System Version
  • Error and Diagnostic Information
  • Notification Token (FCM)

This information is used only for maintaining and improving the performance and security of the App.

5. Data Security

Protecting your information is important to us.

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational security measures to help safeguard your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures include:

  • Secure Authentication
  • Encrypted Communication (HTTPS)
  • Controlled Database Access
  • Secure Cloud Storage
  • Access Restrictions for Authorized Systems

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Maintain your account.
  • Provide booking services.
  • Maintain booking history.
  • Process subscriptions.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our policies.

When an account is permanently deleted, associated personal information will be removed or anonymized where reasonably possible, unless retention is required by law.

7. Your Privacy Rights

As a Buzly user, you may have the right to:

  • Access your account information.
  • Update your personal information.
  • Change your profile details.
  • Delete your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Contact us regarding privacy-related concerns.

We will make reasonable efforts to respond to legitimate privacy requests in accordance with applicable laws.

8. Account Deletion

Buzly allows users to permanently delete their account.

When you choose to delete your account:

  • Your account will be permanently removed from Buzly.
  • Your personal information associated with the account will be deleted from our systems where applicable.
  • You will no longer be able to access your account or its features.
  • This action cannot be undone.

Certain information may be retained if required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our agreements.

9. Children's Privacy

Buzly is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information.

If you believe that a child under the age of 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • New features or services.
  • Changes in legal or regulatory requirements.
  • Improvements to our privacy practices.
  • Security enhancements.

When we make significant changes, we will update the Effective Date shown at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

Your continued use of the Buzly App after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way your information is handled, please contact us:

  • Developer: G. Vasu
  • Application: Buzly
  • Support Email: support@buzly.in

We will make reasonable efforts to respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.

12. Consent

By downloading, installing, creating an account, or using the Buzly App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please discontinue the use of the App.

Summary

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Buzly mobile application, including:

  • Customers
  • Shop Owners

It explains:

  • What information we collect.
  • How we use your information.
  • When information may be shared.
  • How we protect your data.
  • Your rights regarding your personal information.
  • How to contact us with privacy-related questions.

Buzly is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly, securely, and transparently.
