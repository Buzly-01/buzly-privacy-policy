# Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** July 30, 2026 Welcome to **Buzly** ("Buzly", "we", "our", or "us"). Buzly is a local marketplace and booking platform that connects customers with nearby businesses such as salons, beauty parlours, tattoo studios, footwear stores, and other local shops. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Buzly mobile application ("App"). By creating an account or using Buzly, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App. --- # 1. Information We Collect We collect information that is necessary to provide our services, improve the user experience, maintain platform security, and operate the Buzly marketplace. ## 1.1 Customer Information When a customer creates an account, we may collect: * Full Name * Email Address * Profile Photo (if uploaded) * Authentication information through Google Sign-In or Email OTP * Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for notifications * Booking history * Account creation date and related account information **Important:** Buzly does **not** store your password in its own database. Authentication credentials are securely managed through our authentication provider (Supabase Authentication). --- ## 1.2 Shop Owner Information When a business owner registers on Buzly, we may collect: * Owner Name * Email Address * Shop Name * Shop Address * City * District * Shop Location * Business Opening Time * Business Closing Time * Services Offered * Service Prices * Service Duration * Shop Banner Image * Shop Profile Image * Subscription information * Account creation and update timestamps This information is used to create and manage the shop profile displayed to customers. --- ## 1.3 Booking Information When customers make bookings, Buzly collects information such as: * Selected Shop * Selected Services * Booking Date * Booking Time * Booking Status * Booking History * Total Booking Amount (if applicable) This information helps both customers and shop owners manage appointments efficiently. --- ## 1.4 Images and Media Users may upload images, including: ### Customers * Profile Photo ### Shop Owners * Shop Profile Image * Shop Banner Image Uploaded images are securely stored and displayed only for the intended purpose within the Buzly platform. --- ## 1.5 Location Information Buzly **does not collect or track customers' live location.** For shop owners, we collect only the shop's location provided during shop registration. This location is displayed to customers so they can easily find the business. We do not continuously track the owner's device location. --- ## 1.6 Notifications Buzly uses push notifications to provide important updates, including: * Booking updates * Booking confirmations * Booking status changes * Important account-related notifications * Service-related announcements Users can manage notification permissions through their device settings. --- ## 1.7 Payment Information Buzly offers subscription plans for shop owners using **Razorpay**. For subscription payments, payment processing is handled securely by Razorpay. Buzly may store limited payment-related information such as: * Razorpay Order ID * Razorpay Payment ID * Subscription Plan * Payment Status * Subscription Start Date * Subscription Expiry Date **Buzly does not store debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, CVV, or bank account credentials.** All payment information is processed securely by Razorpay in accordance with their security standards. --- ## 1.8 Information Collected Automatically To improve the reliability and security of the App, Buzly may automatically collect certain technical information, including: * Device information * App version * Operating system version * Error and diagnostic information * Notification token (FCM) This information is used only for maintaining and improving the performance and security of the App. # Privacy Policy (Part 2) ## 2. How We Use Your Information Buzly uses the information we collect to provide, maintain, improve, and secure our services. Specifically, we use your information to: * Create and manage your Buzly account. * Authenticate users securely using Google Sign-In or Email OTP. * Enable customers to browse shops and services. * Allow customers to book appointments with shop owners. * Allow shop owners to manage their shop profiles, services, bookings, and subscription plans. * Display shop information to customers. * Send booking confirmations, reminders, cancellations, and important account notifications. * Process owner subscription payments. * Prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and misuse of the platform. * Improve the performance, security, and reliability of the App. * Comply with applicable legal obligations. We only use your information for purposes related to operating and improving the Buzly platform. --- # 3. How We Share Your Information Buzly values your privacy. We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes. Your information may be shared only in the following situations: ### With Shop Owners When you create a booking, the relevant shop owner may receive information necessary to complete your appointment, such as: * Your name * Booking date and time * Selected services * Booking status Only information required to provide the booked service is shared. --- ### With Customers Information entered by shop owners may be displayed publicly within the Buzly App, including: * Shop Name * Shop Address * City * District * Shop Location * Business Hours * Services * Prices * Shop Images This information is displayed so customers can discover and book local businesses. --- ### Legal Requirements We may disclose information if required by law or when necessary to: * Comply with legal obligations. * Protect the rights and safety of Buzly, our users, or the public. * Prevent fraud, abuse, or illegal activities. --- # 4. Third-Party Services Buzly relies on trusted third-party service providers to operate the App. ### Supabase Supabase provides services including: * User Authentication * Secure Database * Cloud Storage * Backend Infrastructure Authentication credentials are securely managed through Supabase Authentication. --- ### Google Sign-In Users may choose to sign in using their Google account. If you use Google Sign-In, Google may provide Buzly with limited account information, such as: * Name * Email Address * Profile Picture (if available) Only the information necessary for authentication is accessed. --- ### Razorpay Owner subscription payments are processed through Razorpay. Razorpay securely handles payment processing. Buzly does **not** store: * Credit Card Numbers * Debit Card Numbers * CVV * UPI PIN * Internet Banking Passwords * Bank Account Credentials Payment information is handled according to Razorpay's own privacy and security practices. --- ### Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Buzly uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications, including: * Booking confirmations * Booking updates * Subscription notifications * Important service announcements FCM tokens are used only for sending notifications and are not shared for advertising purposes. --- # 5. Data Security Protecting your information is important to us. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational security measures to help safeguard your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include: * Secure Authentication * Encrypted communication (HTTPS) * Controlled database access * Secure cloud storage * Access restrictions for authorized systems While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security. --- # 6. Data Retention We retain your information only for as long as necessary to: * Maintain your account. * Provide booking services. * Maintain booking history. * Process subscriptions. * Meet legal and regulatory obligations. * Resolve disputes and enforce our policies. When an account is permanently deleted, associated personal information will be removed or anonymized where reasonably possible, unless retention is required by law. --- # 7.
