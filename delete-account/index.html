Buzly Delete Account

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

This page explains how Buzly customers and shop owners can request account deletion, what to expect after confirming a request, and why some information may need to be retained.

1. Introduction

Buzly provides a way for customers to discover businesses and book appointments, and for shop owners to manage their business presence and bookings. If you no longer wish to use Buzly, you can request deletion of your account using the relevant option in the app.

Account deletion is different from simply signing out or uninstalling the application. Uninstalling Buzly does not itself submit an account-deletion request.

2. Customer Account Deletion

To request deletion of a customer account in the Buzly app:

  1. Open Buzly and sign in to the customer account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the information shown in the app and select Confirm only if you want to proceed.

If you do not see this option in your installed version of Buzly, contact support@buzly.in for assistance.

3. Shop Owner Account Deletion

To request deletion of a shop owner account in the Buzly app:

  1. Open Buzly and sign in to the shop owner account you want to delete.
  2. Open Owner Shop Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the information shown in the app and select Confirm only if you want to proceed.

If you have an active or past subscription, review any relevant subscription information before proceeding. Account deletion should not be assumed to cancel or erase records held by a third-party payment provider.

4. What Happens After You Confirm?

Confirming the in-app action submits or proceeds with the deletion flow available in your installed version of Buzly. The exact effects depend on the app and backend implementation.

Do not assume that confirming deletion removes every record associated with you from all systems. This webpage does not independently delete accounts or data, and it cannot confirm the completion status of an individual request.

If you need confirmation of the status of a request, contact support@buzly.in from the email address associated with your account where possible. Buzly may need to verify that you control the account before discussing or processing a request.

5. What Happens If You Cancel?

If you cancel or back out before confirming the deletion request, you have not confirmed the request through that screen. Your account may remain available, subject to its existing status and the normal operation of Buzly.

If you are unsure, leave the screen without confirming and contact support before proceeding.

6. Information That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal purposes. Depending on the records held and applicable requirements, this may include:

  • Booking history or records needed to manage appointment-related disputes and business operations.
  • Shop, owner, or subscription-related records needed for business, accounting, or support purposes.
  • Transaction references or payment records held by payment providers, including Razorpay.
  • Security, fraud-prevention, or technical logs where retention is appropriate or required.

This list describes categories that may be retained; it does not confirm that every category is stored, nor that every record is deleted or retained in every case. Information handled by Google, Firebase, Razorpay, or other third parties may be subject to their own systems and policies. Buzly cannot promise deletion from third-party systems through this webpage.

Any retained information should be handled in accordance with applicable requirements and the Buzly Privacy Policy.

7. Requesting Deletion Outside the App

If you cannot access your account or cannot use the in-app option, email support@buzly.in from the email address associated with your Buzly account where possible. Include that you want to request account deletion and identify whether you are a customer or shop owner.

Support may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership before processing or responding to the request. Do not email your password, one-time passcodes, UPI PIN, card details, or other sensitive authentication or payment credentials.

Important:

Email is a support contact route, not an automated deletion form. Sending an email does not by itself confirm that deletion has been completed. You may contact support to ask about the status of your request.
