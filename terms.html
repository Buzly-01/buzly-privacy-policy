8. QR Verification Policy

Buzly uses a QR-based verification system to confirm that Customers have arrived for their scheduled appointments.

8.1 QR Availability

The booking QR Code becomes available to the Customer approximately 15 minutes before the scheduled appointment time.

The QR Code is intended solely for appointment verification and must only be presented at the booked Shop during the scheduled appointment.

8.2 Verification Process

  • The Customer presents the QR Code to the Shop Owner.
  • The Shop Owner scans the QR Code using the Buzly application.
  • Successful scanning confirms the appointment.
  • The booking status is updated as verified within the Platform.

8.3 Verification Deadline

If the QR Code is not successfully scanned and verified within approximately 5 minutes after the scheduled appointment time, the booking may be automatically cancelled by the Platform.

This verification process helps improve booking accuracy and reduces missed or fraudulent appointments.

Important:

Customers are responsible for arriving on time and presenting the QR Code for verification. Failure to complete QR verification within the permitted time may result in automatic cancellation of the booking.
